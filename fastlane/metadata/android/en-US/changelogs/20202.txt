Homer's first release on F-Droid.

It is the same app as Homer 2.2.2 on GitHub, without the built-in updater: F-Droid keeps it up to date.

Coming from the GitHub build? F-Droid signs Homer with its own key, so uninstall that one first. If you sync your progress, it comes back from your Nextcloud when you sign in again; downloads have to be fetched again.
